Our Story

From Small Beginnings to Indonesia's Leading Travel Agency

VSTravel was founded in 2020 by a group of passionate travelers who saw an opportunity to create a travel agency that truly understood the needs of modern explorers. Starting as a small office in Jakarta with just five employees, we focused on creating personalized travel experiences that went beyond the typical tourist attractions.

Despite launching during a challenging time for the travel industry, our commitment to quality service and authentic experiences quickly earned us a loyal customer base. By the end of our first year, we had served over 500 satisfied travelers and expanded our team to 15 dedicated professionals.

In 2021, we opened our second office in Medan to better serve customers in Sumatra, followed by our Surabaya branch in 2022 and Bandung location in 2023. Today, VSTravel employs over 50 travel experts across our four locations, and we've helped more than 10,000 travelers create unforgettable memories.

Our journey continues as we expand our offerings while maintaining the personalized touch that has become our hallmark. At VSTravel, we're not just selling trips – we're crafting experiences that last a lifetime.
